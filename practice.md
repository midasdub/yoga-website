A running list — less a curriculum than a set of things my body seems to need.
It changes; that's the point.

## Most mornings

- **Ten breaths standing still** before anything else. Feet planted, eyes shut.
- **Cat–cow and a slow sun salutation A**, three rounds, no counting.
- **Adho mukha svanasana** held long enough to stop negotiating with it.
- **Virabhadrasana I & II**, right side always stiffer than the left.
- **Savasana**, five minutes, non-negotiable.

## Working towards

- **Pincha mayurasana** — forearm stand, at the wall, shoulders honest.
- **Hanumanasana** — front splits. A multi-year project.
- **Nadi shodhana** for ten unbroken minutes without drifting off.

## On the hard days

Legs up the wall. Supported child's pose. That's a whole practice.

> The posture you can actually do today is the real posture.
