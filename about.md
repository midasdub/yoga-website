I'm {{ site.author }}. I started practising yoga because my back hurt and I
couldn't sit still, and I kept going for reasons that turned out to have very
little to do with either.

This site isn't a class schedule or a teaching business. It's a notebook. I
write down what happened on the mat so that six months from now I can look back
and see the shape of it.

## How I practise

Mornings, mostly at home, on a mat that has seen things. Slow vinyasa during
the week, yin on Sundays, and a pranayama practice that I am always either
devoted to or quietly avoiding.

## Get in touch

If something here was useful, or you want to compare notes on a posture that
refuses to cooperate, write to me at
