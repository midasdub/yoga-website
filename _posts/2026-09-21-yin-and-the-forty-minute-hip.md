Long yin session. Dragon pose, five minutes a side, which is four and a half
minutes longer than I want it to be.

Somewhere in minute three the sensation stopped being sharp and became
something flatter and wider — less like pain, more like weather. The teacher
said something I keep turning over:

> Don't go to your edge. Go to where you can still breathe, and then wait for
> the edge to come to you.

Walked home feeling about two inches taller and slightly emotional, which is
apparently what hips do.
