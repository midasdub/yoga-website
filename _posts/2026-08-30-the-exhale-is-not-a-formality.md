---
title: "The exhale is not a formality"
date: 2026-08-30 07:15:00 +0300
tags: [pranayama, breath]
place: Home, kitchen floor
---

Counted my breath properly for the first time in weeks and found the ratio was
all wrong. Four in, two out. I've been treating the exhale as the boring part
you get through to reach the next inhale.

Spent twenty minutes just lengthening it. Four in, six out. By the tenth round
my shoulders had come down from somewhere near my ears, which I hadn't noticed
they were doing.

Note to self: when a posture feels like a fight, I am almost certainly holding
my breath.
