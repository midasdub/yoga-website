Pincha mayurasana again, at the wall. The shoulders are willing now, which is
new — for most of last year they simply weren't. What's missing is the middle:
ribs flaring, low back doing the work the abdomen should be doing.

What helped today: dolphin pose, held for a full minute, walking the feet in
until the hips stacked. Boring. Effective.

Three attempts. Two collapses, one hold of maybe four seconds where everything
lined up and I could feel it — the whole body as a single column instead of a
negotiation between parts.

That's the thing I'm chasing. Not the shape. The feeling of the shape being
easy.
